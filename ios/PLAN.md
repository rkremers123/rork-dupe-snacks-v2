# Dupe Snacks — Gluten-Free Snack Finder App


## What is Dupe Snacks?
A beautiful dark-themed app for people with celiac disease to discover gluten-free alternatives ("dupes") to popular snacks, with direct Amazon purchase links and smart filtering.

---

### **Features**

- **Browse snacks** by scrolling through curated gluten-free alternatives organized by category
- **Search** for specific snacks by name (e.g. "Oreos") with instant results
- **Filter products** by allergens (Soy, Dairy, Nuts, Eggs, Corn), price range, gluten-free certification, and category (Cookies, Chips, Crackers, Bars, Candy, Cereal)
- **Sort results** by Popular, Top Rated, or Price (Low/High)
- **View product details** including description, allergen badges, certification status, star ratings, and reviews
- **Save favorites** with a heart button — view them all in one place
- **Buy on Amazon** — magenta call-to-action button opens the Amazon product link directly

---

### **Design**

- **Dark navy theme** (#1A1A2E background) with teal (#2DD4BF) and magenta (#E91E8C) accents
- Product cards with images, bold white text, teal brand labels, yellow star ratings, and magenta Buy buttons
- Heart icon on every card to toggle favorites
- Red allergen warning badges and green "✓ Gluten-Free Certified" badge on detail pages
- Smooth spring animations and haptic feedback on interactions
- Clean, modern feel inspired by premium food and shopping apps

---

### **Screens**

1. **Browse (Home)** — Horizontally scrollable category sections, each showing product cards with image, name, brand, price, rating, Buy button, and favorite toggle
2. **Search** — Search bar at top with filter chips for categories and allergens, plus a price range slider; results update in real-time
3. **Favorites** — Grid of all saved favorite products, with easy access to details or purchase
4. **Product Detail** — Large hero image, full product info, allergen badges, GF certification badge, star rating, description, and a prominent "Buy on Amazon" button

---

### **Navigation**

- Bottom tab bar with 3 tabs: **Browse** (house icon, teal when active), **Search** (magnifying glass, magenta when active), **Favorites** (heart, magenta when active)

---

### **App Icon**

- Dark navy background with a gluten-free wheat symbol crossed out, accented with teal and magenta, modern and clean
